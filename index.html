Notes from Netta

I’ve always been curious about how things work.

As a kid, that meant carrying around a sketchbook and trying to recreate anything that caught my eye. Over time, drawing turned into designing interfaces, leading teams, starting companies, formulating skincare, and, more recently, writing software.

Looking back, the medium has changed far more than the motivation. I’ve always been happiest making thoughtful things that help people in some way.

Leaving tech to build Domingo taught me that the work I love isn’t tied to an industry. It’s the process of building something from nothing, learning relentlessly, and caring deeply about the people on the other side.

Building Matta reminded me how much I missed making software. More importantly, it reminded me that I’m happiest when I’m learning, making, and sharing what I discover along the way.
